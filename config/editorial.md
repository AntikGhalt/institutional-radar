# Editorial policy

The calendar and the digest have different purposes. Preserve all dated candidates from configured public sources in the full-calendar attachment, with provenance and verification status. The email is a selective economic agenda, not an exhaustive list. The policy is qualitative, not a whitelist, fixed quota or keyword filter.

## Core interests
- Labour-market economics: employment, unemployment, hours, flows, contractual and actual wages, real wages, labour costs, productivity, collective bargaining, structural change and labour policy; Italian data and meaningful international comparisons. Labour productivity is especially important.
- Households and consumers: consumption expenditure, purchasing power, inflation, household income, savings, and consumer/business confidence; recurring monthly or quarterly indicators are useful.
- Market services and trade: retail, turnover and value added in industry and services, tourism and accommodation (including nights spent), sectoral employment and business demography.
- Major economic and demographic indicators: quarterly national accounts/GDP, employment in national accounts, resident population/demographic dynamics, structural population reports, important R&D and innovation statistics.
- Poverty: absolute poverty in Italy and methodological developments toward European absolute poverty measures are core interests. Relative poverty, material deprivation, living conditions and inequality matter when substantive or methodologically informative, but are generally secondary.
- New substantial institutional reports, surveys, datasets and methodological results may qualify even if not covered above. Avoid unrelated administrative releases, narrow sector figures and ceremonial events.

## Selection
Use high relevance for major/core releases, medium for useful secondary context. Only include an event in review.events if it merits space in the editorial digest; use the raw attachment for the rest. Week 1 can include important medium-priority context; weeks 2–3 should be more selective, days 22–30 only genuinely useful advance notice. Do not add filler or impose rigid numerical quotas. Avoid duplicating different publication formats of the same substantive release. Distinguish date certainty from topic relevance. Keep reasons short and economically specific, Italian prose, original publication title when useful. Prefer clearly identifiable statistics, reports or presentations; do not silently drop major new topics. The full attachment is comprehensive only for what the pipeline actually retrieved, not every event at every institution.
