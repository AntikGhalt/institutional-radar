# Architecture and operating model

Each Sunday is identified by YYYY-MM-DD. The collector writes an immutable collection.json. Claude creates a data-only review PR; trusted main validates its schema and collection hash. ChatGPT independently searches official sources and accepts the reviewed JSON after checks, recording audit.json with exact blob hashes. The finalizer derives operational state from current artifacts and trusted PR metadata. A validated open Claude PR can be read as data only when main lacks a review; it is not executed or automatically merged. A missing audit prevents ICS export, not delivery. A missing collection yields a health-only report, not stale data.

health.json records stage statuses and consecutive weeks without verified handoff, based on the immediately previous week's recorded health. No prior record means no evidence of earlier failures; this is not telemetry of external AI service quotas. Full-calendar.csv combines retrieved dated candidates and reviewed selections, with exact normalized-title deduplication (different title variants may remain). The curated digest is controlled by config/editorial.md. SMTP reservations bind output bytes and prevent silent retries of ambiguous sends. Secrets are injected by Actions only. The second email for newly announced releases and cross-week editorial change tracking are separate future work.

Calendar-only research entries are preserved in the CSV without entering the digest. Calendar draft links create editable all-day events (no account access); unverified CSV entries are explicitly labelled. Individual ICS files for audited confirmed selected entries are published with the delivery reservation, while the bulk ICS remains restricted to high relevance. The same UID is used in both formats. Prior sent weeks are immutable; a scheduled retry of a sent digest is a no-op for SMTP.

Editorial numbering is continuous across time windows. Novelty compares normalized institution/title/date with the most recent sent, nonempty digest; exact selection manifests are stored with reservations. Legacy sent editions use their preserved review as a baseline. A changed date or materially changed title can look new; cancellation/rescheduling detection is a separate future feature. Test emails are explicitly labelled, require reviewed live data, and do not write production receipts or health. Manual preview is read-only. Future production delivery is blocked; the premature 2026-12-06 diagnostic test is archived outside the weekly directories.
